## The one-line philosophy

> "Girls don't want you to be normal." / "Girls hate you because you're boring."
> / "It's all about being confident, bro."

Every reel opens with one of those three lines. The whole method is: **be
unexpected, stay unbothered, and close fast.**

## The 5-step structure (it's the same in almost every chat)

| Step | What he does | Example pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Weird setup** | Opens with a strange statement or comparison that *forces* a "wym?" / "how so?" | "You're like [historical figure / random object]" · "I'm like a horse in tall grass" · "Do you brush your teeth?" |
| 1b. **…or bait-and-switch** | Opens with a *normal* saying she'll agree with, then flips it once she agrees. This is the opener from his #1 reel (180K views). He ran it on two matches in the same video to prove it wasn't a fluke | "They say history repeats itself" → "I believe that" → [cheeky reason he hopes it repeats] |
| 2. **Pun payoff** | Lands a punchline (usually a double meaning) on her follow-up question | horse → "I'm just *tickled* to be here" |
| 3. **Run the bit** | Doesn't change topic. Stays in the same theme and builds on whatever *she* adds | Jail joke → she adds a line → he keeps the cop/criminal roleplay going for 4 more texts |
| 4. **Match, then raise** | Turns up the flirting only after she plays along ("Promise?", "I wouldn't mind", "lol sure") | One notch at a time, never a jump |
| 5. **Close immediately** | The moment she's warm, two words: **"Phone number"** (no "would you maybe…") | Or turns the bit into the date ask: "so when we [bit-as-a-verb]?" |
| 5b. **"So yea?" check** | When she's flirting but hasn't clearly said yes, he sends just "so yea?". On her "yes", he sends "phone number" | Used in both chats of his #1 reel |

## Handling tests and attitude (his "toughest matchup" reel)

When she's cold, sarcastic, or says "lame, ciao":

- **Never apologize or explain.** He doesn't fold or chase.
- **Deadpan one-word answers** to challenges: "are you talking back to me?" → "Yes".
  "are you gonna track me?" → "Yes".
- **Dry sarcasm that flips the frame**: she's rude → "Oh what a kind response to a compliment".
- **Turn insults into compliments to yourself**: "I hate you" → (paraphrasing the REF demo) "you spelled 'obsessed with me' wrong".
- **Push-pull:** a sincere-sounding compliment ("you seem like a lot of fun to be around") right after being teased reopens a chat that looked dead.
- She said "lame ciao" and he *still* got the number. Dead-looking chats aren't dead if you stay unbothered.

## Texting mechanics

- **Short.** Most of his messages are under ~10 words. Lowercase is fine.
- **One text at a time.** No paragraphs, no double-texting walls.
- **Skip boring questions.** When she opens with "what's your favorite music?", he ignores it and goes with a bold line.
- **"Ask nicely"** → he goes along with it playfully ("Phone number please") instead of getting defensive.
- **Close on momentum.** He asks for the number on her *warmest* message, not three messages later.

## Profile tips (from the "We gotta max the profile" reel)

- Lead with your single best photo: a bright, genuine smile with good light and some energy.
- Cut low-effort selfies: bathroom mirror, harsh light, parking garages.
- No duplicates. Each photo should show a different setting or outfit.

## What Wingman deliberately does differently

His content is heavy on sexual innuendo. Wingman can match that energy (set
**Spice → Spicy**) but with two guardrails that are just good game:

- **It only raises the flirting after she has.** It reads her messages and matches her level.
- **When she says no, seems uncomfortable, or says she's not interested, it doesn't push.**
  It suggests a smooth, confident exit or a change of topic. Pushing past a no is the
  opposite of the "unbothered" frame, and it's how you get unmatched or reported.
